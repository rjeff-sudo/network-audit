Core capabilities

Everything you need to
stay secure

SME-Shield combines network discovery, port scanning, CVE intelligence, and plain-English reporting into one tool built for the realities of Kenyan business.

Network Discovery

Automatically detects your subnet and sweeps every IP address, returning every active device — computers, printers, routers, and IoT devices — with hostnames resolved.

Banner Grabbing

Connects to each open port and reads the service banner to extract the exact product name and version number — turning "port 80 open" into "nginx 1.18.0".

CVE Intelligence

Cross-references every discovered service against the NIST National Vulnerability Database in real time, pulling CVSS scores and descriptions for each known weakness.

Risk Scoring

Translates raw CVE data into a single 0–100 security score using a logarithmic algorithm. Critical vulnerabilities impact more than minor ones, with diminishing returns to avoid panic scoring.

PDF Reports

Generates a downloadable PDF with plain-English explanations any business owner can act on — score, findings, and prioritised fix advice — in the EAT timezone.

Remediation Advice

Every CVE comes with a specific, actionable fix — not just "update your software" but exactly which version to install and what setting to change, with 30+ curated entries.

Simple by design

From network to
report in four steps

No configuration files. No command line. Open the dashboard, click a button, and receive a full security audit in minutes.

Step 01

Detect your network

SME-Shield reads your machine's network interface and automatically calculates the correct subnet — no manual IP configuration required.

Step 02

Discover devices

A concurrent worker pool sweeps every IP in the subnet, probing common ports to find active hosts — routers, servers, printers, cameras — and resolves their hostnames.

Step 03

Deep audit

Select any device and SME-Shield scans 23 ports, grabs service banners, queries the NVD for CVEs, and streams every finding live to your dashboard as it is discovered.

Step 04

Download your report

Receive a branded PDF with your security score, open services, CVE findings, and plain-English recommendations — ready to share with your IT person or print for a client.
